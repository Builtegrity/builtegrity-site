Builtegrity Fee Schedule

Draft for licensed payments/corporate counsel and tax review. This draft cannot authorize live fees until its legal-document version and the corresponding fee/refund policy are approved in Builtegrity.

Simple model

Builtegrity charges only on successfully processed PROTECTED payments. There is no setup fee and no monthly subscription in BG_STANDARD_V1.

Fractional cents

Builtegrity does not round a fractional-cent fee upward on each payment. Amounts smaller than one cent are recorded precisely and carried forward. Once previously earned fractional amounts total a whole cent, that whole cent may be settled on a later successful protected payment.

When the Platform Fee becomes earned

The Builtegrity Platform Fee becomes earned only after the payment succeeds through the Builtegrity-controlled protected route and the processor confirms the matching Builtegrity application fee. Builtegrity records this as the transaction's PROTECTED confirmation. Failed payments, OBSERVED transactions, and IMPORTED records do not create Builtegrity transaction-fee revenue.

Ordinary refunds, cancellations, and disputes

Subject to applicable law, processor requirements, and the jurisdiction-specific rule locked to the transaction, a later merchant/customer refund, partial refund, cancellation, credit, or dispute does not by itself reverse a Platform Fee that was already earned. The merchant's obligation to refund its customer is separate from the merchant's payment for Builtegrity's completed platform service.

Automatic exceptions

Builtegrity will automatically authorize the applicable Platform Fee refund or credit when its approved compliance rules require one. This includes a verified Builtegrity overcharge or duplicate fee, a Platform Fee that was collected when the fee-earning conditions were not satisfied, a refund required by applicable law, or a refund required by binding processor rules. The system may apply a full or proportional Platform Fee refund where the applicable reviewed jurisdiction rule requires it.

Jurisdiction and processor override

Builtegrity's default economic treatment never overrides applicable law or binding processor requirements. Before checkout, the system locks the approved fee/refund policy and applicable reviewed jurisdiction rule to the transaction. If the applicable rule is missing, expired, blocked, or not implemented by a tested automated handler, checkout must fail closed rather than assume Builtegrity may retain the fee.

Processor fees

Stripe processing fees, dispute fees, reserves, refunds, and other processor charges are separate from Builtegrity's Platform Fee and are governed by the merchant's Stripe relationship.

Merchant acknowledgment

Before activation, an authorized merchant owner or administrator must affirmatively accept the current Fee Schedule and separately acknowledge when the Platform Fee becomes earned, its ordinary refund treatment, and the legal/processor/Builtegrity-error exceptions. Builtegrity preserves the accepted document and policy versions as immutable evidence.

Server-owned pricing

The active pricing version is controlled by Builtegrity's server and recorded on each transaction. Merchant/customer callers cannot override pricing or refund-policy fields. A transaction already locked retains its recorded pricing and compliance evidence even after a later policy version is introduced.

Taxes on Builtegrity's service

Where Builtegrity's platform service or transaction fee is taxable, applicable sales/use or similar tax will be separately accounted for under the approved tax method. Builtegrity will not represent collected sales tax as platform revenue. Rhode Island tax registration/treatment remains a launch gate before live RI billing.

Changes

Material pricing or refundability changes require versioning, legal/compliance review, an effective date, and renewed merchant notice/acceptance where required. Historical transactions retain their locked evidence.
