Builtegrity Fee Schedule

Draft for counsel/tax review; no live fee may be charged from this draft until the legal launch gates are approved.

Simple model

Builtegrity intends to charge only when an eligible PROTECTED payment succeeds. There is no setup fee and no monthly subscription in BG_STANDARD_V1.

BG_STANDARD_V1

Each successful PROTECTED payment has the base fee shown below plus 0.10% of the payment amount.

There is no upper Builtegrity fee cap in BG_STANDARD_V1. The percentage component therefore continues to scale with payment value.

Fractional cents

Builtegrity does not round a fractional-cent fee upward on each payment. Amounts smaller than one cent are recorded precisely and carried forward. Once previously earned fractional amounts total a whole cent, that whole cent may be settled on a later successful protected payment. This is intended to make the merchant pay the stated formula rather than a hidden rounding premium.

When a fee is earned

A Builtegrity transaction fee is earned only when the payment succeeds through the Builtegrity-controlled protected route and the matching Stripe application fee is successfully confirmed. Failed payments, OBSERVED transactions, and IMPORTED records do not create Builtegrity transaction-fee revenue.

Refunds and disputes

A later merchant/customer refund, partial refund, or dispute does not by itself reverse an already earned Builtegrity integrity fee because the protected-transaction service has already been delivered. If Builtegrity itself incorrectly calculates or collects its platform fee, the verified excess is eligible for a documented Builtegrity platform-fault credit. An ordinary merchant refund is not a platform-fault credit.

Processor fees

Stripe processing fees, dispute fees, reserves, refunds, and other processor charges are separate from Builtegrity's platform fee and are governed by the merchant's Stripe relationship.

Server-owned pricing

The active pricing version is controlled by Builtegrity's server and recorded on each transaction. Merchant/customer callers cannot override pricing fields. A transaction already priced and locked retains its recorded pricing evidence even after a later pricing version is introduced.

Taxes on Builtegrity's service

Where Builtegrity's platform service or transaction fee is taxable, applicable sales/use or similar tax will be separately accounted for under the approved tax method. Builtegrity will not represent a collected sales tax as platform revenue. Rhode Island tax registration/treatment is a launch gate before live RI billing.

Changes

Material pricing changes require a new pricing version, effective date, and merchant notice/acceptance where required. BG_STANDARD_V1 remains immutable for transactions already created under that version.
