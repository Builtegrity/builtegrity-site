Builtegrity Fee Schedule

Draft for counsel/tax review; no live fee may be charged from this draft.

Fee model

Builtegrity intends to charge a disclosed transaction/application fee only on eligible PROTECTED payments. There is no setup fee or monthly subscription in the V1 model unless a later approved schedule says otherwise.

When a fee is earned

A Builtegrity transaction fee is earned only when the payment succeeds through the Builtegrity-controlled protected route and the matching Stripe application fee is successfully confirmed. OBSERVED and IMPORTED records do not create Builtegrity transaction-fee revenue.

Amount

The production pricing formula is not legally approved in this draft and must be populated from Builtegrity's active server-owned pricing version before merchant acceptance. Callers cannot override pricing fields.

Processor fees

Stripe processing fees, dispute fees, reserves, refunds, and other processor charges are separate from Builtegrity's platform fee and are governed by the merchant's Stripe relationship.

Taxes on Builtegrity's service

Where Builtegrity's platform service or transaction fee is taxable, applicable sales/use or similar tax will be separately accounted for under the approved tax method. Builtegrity will not represent a collected sales tax as platform revenue. Rhode Island tax registration/treatment is a launch gate before live RI billing.

Changes

Material pricing changes require a new version, effective date, and merchant notice/acceptance where required. A transaction already priced and locked will retain its recorded pricing evidence.
