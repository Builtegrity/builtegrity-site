Data Processing Addendum

Draft for counsel review; not effective until approved.

This DPA is intended to govern Builtegrity's processing of personal data on behalf of a merchant where the merchant determines the purposes/means of customer data processing and Builtegrity acts as a service provider/processor for the transaction-record service.

Instructions and purpose

Builtegrity will process merchant-provided personal data only to provide, secure, support, reconcile, document, and comply with law for the Builtegrity service, and as otherwise documented by lawful merchant instructions.

Data and people

Data may include names, email addresses, merchant/customer reference identifiers, purchase/transaction descriptions, contract/consent records, refund/cancellation terms, processor transaction identifiers/events, payout/reconciliation facts, security logs, and support records. Data subjects may include merchant users, customers, partners, and support contacts.

Confidentiality and security

Persons authorized to process personal data must be subject to confidentiality obligations. Builtegrity will maintain reasonable risk-based administrative, technical, and physical security measures, access controls, secrets management, logging, incident response, and vendor oversight.

Subprocessors

Builtegrity may use approved subprocessors listed at /legal/subprocessors/. Builtegrity will impose data-protection/security obligations appropriate to the service and maintain a change process before adding material subprocessors.

Rights requests

Builtegrity will provide reasonable assistance for verified access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out, appeal, or regulator requests where legally required and technically feasible. Deletion may be limited by legal holds, fraud/security, tax/accounting, contract, dispute, or statutory recordkeeping duties.

Return/deletion

At the end of services, Builtegrity will delete or return merchant personal data according to the documented retention schedule and applicable law, except where retention is legally required or subject to legal hold.

Assessments and evidence

Builtegrity will make reasonable compliance/security information available to the merchant and will cooperate with legally required assessments subject to confidentiality, security, and proportionality safeguards.

International transfers

V1 does not support international merchants/customer transactions. Any later cross-border processing requires an approved jurisdiction and transfer-mechanism review before activation.
