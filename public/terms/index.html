Builtegrity · Pre-launch legal draft

Platform Provider Agreement

Draft for licensed-counsel review. This draft cannot be used to activate a live merchant until its legal-document version is marked APPROVED in Builtegrity.

1. Service role

Builtegrity provides transaction-record, payment-context, monitoring, reconciliation, reporting, and related software. The merchant remains the seller and merchant of record for the merchant's goods or services. Stripe, not Builtegrity, processes card payments and provides the connected payment account. Builtegrity does not intend to receive, possess, store, or transmit customer funds.

2. V1 operating scope

V1 is limited to U.S. merchants, U.S. customer transactions, USD, one-time payments, Stripe direct charges on the merchant's connected Stripe account, and automatic Stripe payouts. International activity, subscriptions, marketplace funds routing, manual payouts, escrow/stored value, or Builtegrity custody of funds are disabled unless Builtegrity completes a new legal and technical review.

3. Protected, observed, and imported records

A transaction is designated PROTECTED only when the payment is created through Builtegrity's protected payment route, the payment succeeds, and Builtegrity's transaction fee is successfully attached/confirmed. OBSERVED transactions are detected from processor information but were not created through Builtegrity's protected payment route. IMPORTED transactions are historical/reference data. “Protected” describes Builtegrity's transaction-record state; it is not insurance and does not guarantee payment, refund outcome, dispute outcome, chargeback reversal, or collection.

4. Fees and taxes

The merchant must receive the current Builtegrity Fee Schedule before acceptance. Builtegrity may collect a disclosed application/platform fee through Stripe on eligible protected payments. Applicable sales, use, or similar taxes on Builtegrity's service may be added or otherwise handled as disclosed in the applicable Fee Schedule. Builtegrity will not conceal a tax or material platform fee inside a processor fee.

5. Merchant responsibilities

The merchant is responsible for the lawfulness and accuracy of products/services, customer-facing transaction descriptions, pricing, terms, refund/cancellation policies, fulfillment, required business licenses, sales taxes on the merchant's own sales, customer communications, and honoring applicable consumer-protection obligations. The merchant must not submit prohibited sensitive data to Builtegrity.

6. Stripe account

The merchant must maintain a compatible Stripe connected account, complete Stripe onboarding/KYC, comply with Stripe agreements/restricted-business rules, keep automatic payouts enabled for V1, and authorize Builtegrity to perform the limited connected-account actions required for the service. Stripe may impose its own fees, holds, reserves, disputes, or restrictions independently of Builtegrity.

7. Prohibited and restricted uses

Merchants may not use Builtegrity for illegal activity, sanctioned/prohibited persons or transactions, Stripe-prohibited businesses, child-directed data collection, raw payment-card data, credential collection, money transmission on behalf of others, escrow/stored value, or any use Builtegrity has not approved for V1. See the Acceptable Use Policy.

8. Data and privacy

The merchant must have authority to provide transaction/customer data to Builtegrity and must provide required customer notices. Builtegrity processes merchant/customer data to provide and secure the service as described in the Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum. Merchant-scoped customer records are not combined into a global customer profile for unrelated merchants.

9. Electronic records

By accepting electronically, the merchant agrees to conduct this relationship electronically and to receive/store applicable Builtegrity records electronically, subject to the Electronic Records Consent. Builtegrity versions legal documents and preserves acceptance evidence.

10. Suspension and safety controls

Builtegrity may pause onboarding, payment creation, transaction creation, or account activity when security, sanctions, restricted-business, financial-safety, legal, tax, processor, or compliance controls fail. These fail-closed controls are part of the service.

11. Records and corrections

Builtegrity records processor events based on available evidence. Merchant/customer source data can be inaccurate or incomplete. Builtegrity may append correcting events while preserving historical audit evidence rather than silently rewriting immutable events.

12. Legal provisions still requiring counsel approval

Before live use, Rhode Island counsel/payment counsel must finalize governing-law/venue, warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, indemnification, dispute-resolution procedure, termination effects, survival, notices, and any required statutory consumer/business clauses. Builtegrity's system intentionally will not mark this document APPROVED until that review is completed.
