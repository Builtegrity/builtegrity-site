How your information is used

Bank access is optional and read-only. You never share your bank password with Builtegrity. We use a third-party provider to receive transaction records and match an incoming Stripe/Square deposit to the corresponding whole payout batch. A bank transaction may post later than the payout reported by Stripe or Square.

Builtegrity will not display bank arrival or settlement details without an active authorized account. Changing banks or renewing expired permissions may require you to complete a new authorization. Ambiguous matches remain unverified.

A connected bank feed may create ongoing provider charges. This feature remains disabled until the company approves its pricing, terms and production integration.
