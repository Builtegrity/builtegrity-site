1. Payment processor

Stripe or Square handles your payments; Builtegrity reads events and builds a transaction record. A successful payment does not by itself prove a payout.

Connect the processor through the authorized integration. Do not provide passwords or card numbers.

Open transaction integrity

2. Optional independent bank evidence

Checking your bank settings…

    Bank data limitations: the bank may report transactions hours or days after the processor's payout event; a pending deposit is not a posted deposit; posted does not mean irreversible settlement. Some bank permissions periodically require you to approve access again. Builtegrity cannot renew that permission for you.

    No connection? No misleading bank claims. Bank arrival and posting details are omitted from your PDF unless your approved connection provides verified evidence.

    3. Customer data and custom line items

    Configure your own labels, group customers and record histories, and connect software that sends your additional facts to Builtegrity.

    Start with a customer reference and an invoice/payment reference in your system. Connect these to Builtegrity's customer and TX IDs. Only matched references can be imported—unmatched records are rejected, not guessed.

    Ongoing automation: your existing software must send new records via a configured API, webhook or supported app connector. CSV/JSON is a one-time import unless your system automates future uploads. Arbitrary third-party applications and databases are not connected automatically.

    Configure custom fields & data sources

    4. No daily maintenance

    After setup, Builtegrity automatically receives available processor and optional data-source updates, checks integrity, and refreshes the PDF when opened. The merchant only needs to act if a bank demands renewed consent, a source key is revoked, or their connected service fails. When this happens, the dashboard tells them which permission to fix and links to the exact setting.

    Processor publication, webhook arrival, bank reporting, and Builtegrity processing are different timestamps; none guarantees an exact universal delivery time.
