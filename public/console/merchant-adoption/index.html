What these numbers measure

Eligible merchants are businesses with at least one recorded transaction or a connected processor. A merchant counts as having a profile when it has an active, authorized user membership; a verified owner membership is tracked separately.

Opening an emailed single-transaction link is counted only when the token is valid. Repeat visits and completed owner claims are tracked without recording browser IPs in this adoption report. These numbers describe account usage; they do not independently prove customer satisfaction, product-market fit or the cause of a merchant's choice.

Privacy: this report contains aggregate counts only. It never shows business emails, customer names, refund documents or accountless access tokens.
