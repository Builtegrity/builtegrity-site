THE BUILTEGRITY STANDARD

Built around appropriate matches and honest expectations.

Verification is not a guarantee of future performance. It is a documented process for checking whether a roofing company is eligible for the work before an introduction is made.

Partner eligibility is designed to consider

  • Business identity and authorized contact
  • Applicable registrations or licenses
  • Current insurance documentation
  • Service area and roofing capabilities
  • Availability and project fit
  • Conduct, communication and outcome history

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what happens before you submit.

Is Builtegrity a roofing contractor?

No. Builtegrity organizes, reviews and coordinates roofing requests. The roofing professional is responsible for inspection, technical advice, pricing, contracts and construction.

Will my information be sent to several companies?

Builtegrity’s launch process is designed around one active partner introduction at a time—not a broad distribution of your request.

Am I required to hire the roofing company?

No. Submitting a request does not obligate you to accept an inspection, estimate or roofing contract.

What if I do not know what kind of roof I have?

That is completely fine. Select “Not sure” and tell us what you are seeing. A roofing professional must make any technical determination.

Can I mention siding, gutters or another exterior need?

Yes. The request includes a place to identify related exterior needs. Availability depends on the assigned company’s capabilities.
