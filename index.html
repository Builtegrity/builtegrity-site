BUILTEGRITY | PRODUCT PREVIEW

Know what happened to every payment.

Keep Stripe or Square. Builtegrity organizes the details around payments made through your Builtegrity checkout: customer terms and acceptance, payment confirmation, application fees, refunds and payout progress.

Less chasing records. More running your business.

Payment processors provide payment, refund, dispute and payout reporting. But business owners still need to connect a payment to the work, agreement and customer behind it. Builtegrity brings your payment evidence into one searchable transaction record.

  • Customer agreement: know which terms were presented and accepted before payment.
  • Payment and fees: see whether expected processor events and application fees were recorded.
  • Payout trail: distinguish a successful charge from a payment accounted for in a reported payout.
  • Exceptions: see what's missing or needs attention instead of hunting through records.
  • Printable proof: open the latest transaction PDF for your records or to share.

Designed for a local integrity recheck every 2 minutes and a watchdog review every 10 minutes after the Cloudflare application is deployed. Processor reporting remains subject to Stripe/Square event availability.
