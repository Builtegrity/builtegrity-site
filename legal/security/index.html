Pre-launch security disclosure

Security Summary

Draft for legal/security review; not a certification or guarantee.

Builtegrity is designed around data minimization and fail-closed controls. Stripe-hosted Checkout collects card credentials; Builtegrity is designed not to receive raw card numbers or CVC/CVV data.

Identity and access

Administrative access uses authenticated identity, tenant-specific authorization, least-privilege roles, restricted admin surfaces, same-origin protections, and revocable access records.

Secrets and encryption

Provider secrets are kept outside source control. Sensitive accounting OAuth tokens are encrypted before database storage. Public transaction links use unguessable tokens rather than exposing administrative credentials.

Integrity and audit evidence

Transaction events and key financial/legal evidence are append-only or immutable after confirmation. Processor webhooks use cryptographic signature validation and idempotent receipt tracking. Legal documents and acceptances are versioned.

Monitoring

Automated watchdogs monitor failed/stuck processor events, transaction/merchant state, financial safety, reporting, tax readiness, and other configured dependencies. Critical controls can pause onboarding or transaction/payment creation.

Resilience

Builtegrity maintains recovery/owner-independence documentation, durable evidence storage, accounting snapshots, CI gates, and operational runbooks. Production recovery controls must be tested before LIVE.

Incident response

Suspected security events enter a documented incident workflow covering containment, investigation, evidence preservation, legal notification analysis, remediation, and post-incident review. State-specific notification timing is evaluated based on affected residents.

Vendors

Providers that can receive personal/financial data require contract, security, subprocessor, and periodic-review evidence before Builtegrity can reach LIVE legal readiness.

PCI

Builtegrity intends to maintain the applicable PCI DSS scope for a Stripe-hosted/redirected e-commerce integration, including the applicable SAQ/scan obligations. PCI status is a hard launch gate.
