Electronic Records & Signature Consent

Draft for counsel review; not effective until approved.

By affirmatively accepting this consent, the merchant agrees to conduct the Builtegrity relationship electronically, use electronic signatures/clickwrap where applicable, and receive Builtegrity agreements, notices, statements, transaction records, reports, and related records electronically.

Access

The merchant should retain copies of important records. Builtegrity intends to make relevant records downloadable or otherwise reproducible and to preserve versioned acceptance evidence.

Hardware/software

Access generally requires an internet connection, a current standards-compliant browser capable of displaying HTML/PDF content, an email account for notices, and the ability to save or print electronic files.

Paper and withdrawal

Where applicable law requires a right to withdraw electronic consent or request paper copies for a legally required consumer writing, Builtegrity will provide the required process and disclose any fee before the consent becomes effective. Merchant platform administration is designed to be electronic-first.

Keeping contact information current

Merchants must keep account email/contact information current so legally required or operational notices can be delivered.

Specific statutory disclosures

This general consent does not replace any enhanced E-SIGN consent procedure required when a separate law requires a consumer disclosure to be provided “in writing.” Builtegrity must identify those disclosures and apply the required affirmative-consent flow before relying on electronic delivery.
