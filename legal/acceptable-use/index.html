Pre-launch legal draft

Acceptable Use Policy

Draft for counsel review; not effective until approved.

Builtegrity V1 may be used only by approved U.S. businesses for lawful U.S. customer transactions denominated in USD and processed through the approved Stripe direct-charge flow.

Prohibited uses

Do not use Builtegrity for illegal goods/services; fraud; deceptive or abusive practices; sanctions evasion; transactions involving blocked persons; Stripe-prohibited businesses; unlicensed regulated financial activity; money transmission for others; escrow/stored value; marketplace fund routing; gambling; unlawful drugs; weapons prohibited by processor rules; counterfeit/stolen goods; sexual exploitation; hate/extremist activity; unlawful surveillance; or any category Builtegrity/Stripe designates prohibited.

Restricted uses

Businesses that Stripe or applicable law treats as restricted may not activate until both Stripe and Builtegrity complete the required review/approval. Builtegrity may require licenses, policies, ownership information, compliance evidence, or enhanced review.

Data restrictions

Do not submit raw card numbers, CVV/CVC, PIN data, Social Security numbers, online-banking credentials, passwords, medical records, biometric templates, or other highly sensitive data that is not expressly required by the approved Builtegrity workflow. Card data must be entered only into Stripe-hosted payment interfaces.

Children

V1 is not child-directed and may not be used to intentionally collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Communications

Do not use Builtegrity to send unlawful spam, robocalls, robotexts, or deceptive communications. Automated marketing SMS/robocalls are disabled in V1.

Enforcement

Builtegrity may block onboarding, suspend activity, preserve evidence, notify processors/providers, or make legally required reports when risk/compliance controls indicate prohibited or unsafe activity. A potential sanctions match must remain blocked until cleared through the approved review process.
