Privacy notice

How Builtegrity collects, uses and shares information connected with a roofing request.

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Draft pending professional legal review

Information we collect

When you submit a request, we collect information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, property address, property type, roofing need, roofing system, project timing, project description, the type of help you request, quote-comparison details and related exterior needs. If you later provide copies of estimates or proposals for comparison, we may also collect those documents and the information they contain. We may also record the source of the request, communications, consent, assignment activity and project-status information.

How we use information

We use information to receive and review requests, confirm project details, evaluate service-area and project fit, communicate with you, coordinate controlled introductions to appropriate roofing professionals, organize documented differences between roofing quotes when requested, maintain records, prevent duplicate or fraudulent requests, respond to complaints and improve Builtegrity’s service.

How we share information

We may share the information reasonably needed to respond to your request with a roofing professional selected for an introduction. We coordinate one selected roofing-company introduction at a time. An additional introduction may be considered only when you request or consent to it and an eligible partner is available. Quote or proposal documents you provide for comparison are used to help organize documented differences. We do not intentionally share another contractor’s proposal with a roofing company unless you ask us to, authorize it or sharing is reasonably necessary to fulfill a specific request you made. We may also use service providers that support hosting, forms, email, data storage, security or business operations. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a properly reviewed business transaction.

Compensation

Builtegrity, its operator or participating representatives may receive compensation connected with a roofing opportunity or resulting project. Compensation does not change your freedom to accept or reject any contractor, estimate or contract. Quote-comparison help is organizational rather than a recommendation of a winning contractor, and Builtegrity may have a compensation relationship with a company involved in an introduction.

Cookies and technical information

The current site may receive basic technical information through its hosting and form providers, such as browser, device, network or request-log information. If analytics or advertising technologies are added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

Data retention and security

We retain information for legitimate operating, recordkeeping, legal, security and dispute-resolution purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to correct information, withdraw permission for future contact or ask a privacy question by emailing hello@builtegrity.com. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security or recordkeeping purposes.

Children

Builtegrity is intended for adults seeking help for a property and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this notice as the service, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the published version.

Contact

Questions may be sent to hello@builtegrity.com.
