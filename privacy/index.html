Privacy Notice

Draft for legal review. This notice is not approved for live merchant onboarding until Builtegrity marks the corresponding legal-document version APPROVED.

What Builtegrity is

Builtegrity is a transaction-record and payment-accountability software layer. Merchants remain the sellers of their goods or services. Stripe processes payment-card transactions. Builtegrity is not a bank and is not intended to hold customer funds.

Information we may process

We may process merchant account and business information, merchant-user identity and authentication references, customer name and email, merchant-supplied purchase and transaction descriptions, terms/refund/cancellation records, electronic-consent evidence, Stripe transaction identifiers and lifecycle events, payout/reconciliation facts, support communications, security logs, and legal/compliance records.

Do not send Builtegrity payment-card numbers, CVC/CVV codes, Social Security numbers, online-banking credentials, passwords, or medical records. Payment credentials must be entered only on Stripe-hosted payment surfaces.

Why we use information

We use information to provide transaction records, create and maintain merchant/customer relationships, document consent and transaction history, reconcile payments and payouts, generate account statements, secure and operate the service, prevent abuse, comply with law and contractual obligations, respond to privacy/security requests, and maintain accounting and tax records.

Service providers

Our current planned service-provider categories include payment processing (Stripe), cloud/application infrastructure (Cloudflare), identity/authentication (Clerk), transactional email (Resend), company accounting (Intuit QuickBooks Online), and source-control/CI infrastructure (GitHub). Our current subprocessor list is available at /legal/subprocessors/.

Sale, targeted advertising, and profiling

Builtegrity's V1 policy is not to sell personal data, not to share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising, and not to use personal data for targeted advertising or legally significant automated profiling. Any future change requires a new legal/compliance review and updated notice/controls before activation.

Retention

We keep information only for a documented business, contractual, security, accounting, tax, dispute, or legal need. Transaction and accounting records may require longer retention than abandoned signup data. Legal holds override scheduled deletion. Builtegrity maintains a written retention schedule that is subject to legal review.

Security

Builtegrity uses layered technical and organizational controls including authenticated administrative access, tenant authorization, signed webhook verification, append-only transaction event records, secret separation, encryption for sensitive integration tokens, monitoring, backup/recovery planning, and incident-response procedures. No security program can guarantee that an incident will never occur.

Your privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, a person may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out, or appeal. Builtegrity intends to accept these requests nationwide even where a particular state threshold has not yet been reached. Requests may be sent to hello@builtegrity.com. We may need to verify identity and may retain data where law, fraud/security prevention, accounting, contract, or legal-defense obligations require it.

Children

Builtegrity V1 is a business-payment product and is not directed to children under 13. Merchants may not intentionally use Builtegrity to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

United States-only V1

Builtegrity V1 is restricted to U.S. merchants, U.S. customer transactions, and USD. International expansion requires a separate jurisdiction review before it can be enabled.

Changes

Material changes will be versioned and dated. Where required, affected users will receive notice or be asked to accept a new version.
