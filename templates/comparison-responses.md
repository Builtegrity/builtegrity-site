# Builtegrity Comparison Response Templates

## Quote comparison acknowledgment

Subject: We received your roofing comparison request

Thanks for sending your Builtegrity request.

You asked for help comparing roofing quotes you already have. We can help organize the documented differences in areas such as scope, materials, allowances, exclusions, warranty terms and pricing structure so you can see what questions still need answers.

If proposal copies would help, reply with the estimates you want reviewed. Please remove or avoid sending bank, payment-card or other unnecessary sensitive financial information.

Builtegrity does not choose a winning contractor, guarantee the lowest price or independently verify every technical statement in a proposal. The hiring decision remains yours.

## Additional-option acknowledgment

Subject: We received your request for another roofing option

Thanks for sending your Builtegrity request.

You asked to discuss another roofing-company option. We will first review the property, project fit and actual partner availability.

Your request is not broadcast to a list of contractors. Any additional roofing-company introduction is considered one at a time and only after you request or consent to that introduction.

A second option is not guaranteed. If another eligible partner is not currently available, we will say so plainly and can still help you organize quotes you obtain independently.
