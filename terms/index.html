The role Builtegrity performs—and the responsibilities that remain with homeowners and roofing professionals.

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Draft pending professional legal review

Builtegrity’s role

Builtegrity provides roofing-request intake, preliminary review, matching and coordination. Builtegrity is not a roofing contractor and does not inspect roofs, diagnose conditions, prepare estimates, determine code compliance, sign roofing contracts, collect contractor deposits, perform construction or provide workmanship or material warranties.

No guaranteed match or outcome

Submitting a request does not guarantee that a roofing professional will accept the request, contact you, provide an inspection or estimate, offer particular pricing, obtain financing approval, begin work or produce a specific result.

Your relationship with the roofing professional

Any inspection, recommendation, estimate, contract, payment, schedule, permit, construction work or warranty is between you and the roofing company. Review the company’s identity, credentials, scope, pricing, contract and insurance information before making a hiring decision.

Your responsibilities

You agree to provide accurate information, submit only requests you are authorized to make, avoid unlawful or fraudulent use, protect any request reference and promptly update material information. You remain free to accept or reject any introduction, inspection, estimate or contract.

Consent to contact and share

When you affirm the consent shown in the request flow, you authorize Builtegrity to contact you about that request and to share the request information with one roofing professional selected to evaluate the opportunity. Communication may occur through the contact method you select. You may withdraw permission for future communications by contacting us.

Launch relationship and compensation

Builtegrity is beginning in Rhode Island through the founder’s existing independent sales relationship with Prime State Roofing. Eligible launch requests may initially be referred to Prime State based on service area, project type and availability. Builtegrity, its operator or participating representatives may receive compensation connected with an opportunity or resulting roofing project.

Emergencies

Builtegrity is not an emergency-response service. Do not rely on the website for immediate protection from active fire, collapse, electrical danger, severe water intrusion or another threat to life or property. Contact the appropriate emergency service and qualified professionals.

Website availability

We may correct, suspend or change the website and request process. Although we work to keep information accurate and the service available, the website is provided on an “as available” basis to the extent permitted by law.
