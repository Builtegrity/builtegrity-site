Builtegrity · Pre-launch legal draft

Platform Provider Agreement

Draft for licensed-counsel review. This draft cannot be used to activate a live merchant until its legal-document version and the applicable Platform Fee/refund policy are marked APPROVED in Builtegrity.

1. Service role

Builtegrity provides transaction-record, payment-context, monitoring, reconciliation, reporting, and related software. The merchant remains the seller and merchant of record for the merchant's goods or services. Stripe, not Builtegrity, processes card payments and provides the connected payment account. Builtegrity does not intend to receive, possess, store, or transmit customer funds.

2. V1 operating scope

V1 is limited to U.S. merchants, U.S. customer transactions, USD, one-time payments, Stripe direct charges on the merchant's connected Stripe account, and automatic Stripe payouts. International activity, subscriptions, marketplace funds routing, manual payouts, escrow/stored value, or Builtegrity custody of funds are disabled unless Builtegrity completes a new legal and technical review.

3. Protected, observed, and imported records

A transaction is designated PROTECTED only when the payment is created through Builtegrity's protected payment route, the payment succeeds, and Builtegrity's transaction fee is successfully attached and processor-confirmed. OBSERVED transactions are detected from processor information but were not created through Builtegrity's protected payment route. IMPORTED transactions are historical/reference data. “Protected” describes Builtegrity's transaction-record state; it is not insurance and does not guarantee payment, refund outcome, dispute outcome, chargeback reversal, or collection.

4. Platform Fees, earning, refunds, and taxes

The merchant must receive the current Builtegrity Fee Schedule before acceptance. Builtegrity charges only on successfully processed PROTECTED payments and may collect the disclosed Platform Fee through Stripe. The Platform Fee becomes earned when the protected payment succeeds and the processor confirms the matching Builtegrity application fee. Subject to applicable law, binding processor requirements, and the jurisdiction-specific rule locked to the transaction, a later merchant/customer refund, partial refund, cancellation, credit, or dispute does not by itself reverse an already-earned Platform Fee.

Builtegrity will automatically authorize the applicable Platform Fee refund or credit when required by the approved compliance rule, including for a verified Builtegrity overcharge or duplicate fee, a fee collected when the fee-earning conditions were not satisfied, or a refund required by applicable law or binding processor requirements. Where an approved jurisdiction rule requires full or proportional Platform Fee refunds after an underlying transaction refund or lost dispute, Builtegrity will apply that rule automatically.

The merchant's obligations to its customer—including refunds, cancellations, fulfillment, and consumer-protection remedies—remain separate from the merchant's obligation to pay Builtegrity for a completed platform service. Applicable sales, use, or similar taxes on Builtegrity's service may be added or otherwise handled as disclosed in the Fee Schedule. Builtegrity will not conceal a tax or material Platform Fee inside a processor fee.

5. Clear disclosure and affirmative merchant acknowledgment

Before activation, an authorized merchant owner or administrator must affirmatively accept the current Platform Agreement and Fee Schedule and separately acknowledge the Platform Fee earning event, ordinary post-earning refund treatment, and automatic legal/processor/Builtegrity-error exceptions. Builtegrity preserves the accepted versions and acknowledgment evidence. If a material fee or refundability term changes, renewed acceptance is required where the approved compliance rule requires it.

6. Merchant responsibilities

The merchant is responsible for the lawfulness and accuracy of products/services, customer-facing transaction descriptions, pricing, terms, refund/cancellation policies, fulfillment, required business licenses, sales taxes on the merchant's own sales, customer communications, and honoring applicable consumer-protection obligations. The merchant must not submit prohibited sensitive data to Builtegrity.

7. Stripe account

The merchant must maintain a compatible Stripe connected account, complete Stripe onboarding/KYC, comply with Stripe agreements/restricted-business rules, keep automatic payouts enabled for V1, and authorize Builtegrity to perform the limited connected-account actions required for the service. Stripe may impose its own fees, holds, reserves, disputes, refunds, or restrictions independently of Builtegrity.

8. Prohibited and restricted uses

Merchants may not use Builtegrity for illegal activity, sanctioned/prohibited persons or transactions, Stripe-prohibited businesses, child-directed data collection, raw payment-card data, credential collection, money transmission on behalf of others, escrow/stored value, or any use Builtegrity has not approved for V1. See the Acceptable Use Policy.

9. Data and privacy

The merchant must have authority to provide transaction/customer data to Builtegrity and must provide required customer notices. Builtegrity processes merchant/customer data to provide and secure the service as described in the Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum. Merchant-scoped customer records are not combined into a global customer profile for unrelated merchants.

10. Electronic records

By accepting electronically, the merchant agrees to conduct this relationship electronically and to receive/store applicable Builtegrity records electronically, subject to the Electronic Records Consent. Builtegrity versions legal documents and preserves acceptance evidence.

11. Suspension and safety controls

Builtegrity may pause onboarding, payment creation, transaction creation, or account activity when security, sanctions, restricted-business, financial-safety, legal, tax, processor, jurisdiction, fee/refund-policy, or compliance controls fail. Unknown or expired Platform Fee refund treatment fails closed rather than being interpreted in Builtegrity's favor.

12. Records and corrections

Builtegrity records processor events based on available evidence. Merchant/customer source data can be inaccurate or incomplete. Builtegrity may append correcting events while preserving historical audit evidence rather than silently rewriting immutable events. Once a transaction's approved fee/refund rule is locked, that rule and its supporting versions remain part of the transaction's historical evidence.

13. Legal provisions still requiring counsel approval

Before live use, Rhode Island counsel/payments counsel must finalize this Platform Fee earning/refund structure and governing-law/venue, warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, indemnification, dispute-resolution procedure, termination effects, survival, notices, and any required statutory consumer/business clauses. Builtegrity's system intentionally will not mark this document or the fee/refund policy APPROVED until that review is completed.
