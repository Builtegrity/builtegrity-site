Builtegrity · Pre-launch legal draft

Platform Provider Agreement

Draft for licensed-counsel review. This draft cannot be used to activate a live merchant until its legal-document version is marked APPROVED in Builtegrity.

Service role

Builtegrity provides transaction-record, payment-context, monitoring, reconciliation, reporting, and related software. The merchant remains the seller and merchant of record. Stripe, not Builtegrity, processes card payments. Builtegrity does not intend to receive, possess, store, or transmit customer funds.

V1 operating scope

V1 is limited to U.S. merchants, U.S. customer transactions, USD, one-time payments, Stripe direct charges on the merchant's connected account, and automatic Stripe payouts. International activity, subscriptions, marketplace funds routing, manual payouts, escrow/stored value, and Builtegrity custody of funds are disabled pending new legal/technical review.

Protected records

PROTECTED is a Builtegrity record state requiring the protected route, successful payment, and confirmed Builtegrity application fee. It is not insurance and does not guarantee payment, refund, dispute, chargeback or collection outcomes.

Fees and taxes

Merchants must receive the approved Fee Schedule before acceptance. Material Builtegrity fees and applicable taxes must be clearly disclosed and separately accounted for.

Merchant responsibilities

Merchants remain responsible for their products/services, pricing, licenses, customer terms, refunds/cancellations, fulfillment, taxes on their own sales, communications and consumer-law duties.

Stripe

Merchants must maintain a compatible Stripe connected account and comply with Stripe agreements and restricted-business rules. Stripe may impose its own fees, disputes, reserves or restrictions independently.

Data and electronic records

Merchants must have authority to provide transaction/customer data. Builtegrity processes data under the approved Privacy Notice/DPA and preserves versioned electronic acceptance evidence.

Safety controls

Builtegrity may pause onboarding/payment/transaction activity when security, sanctions, restricted-business, financial, legal, tax, processor or compliance controls fail.

Counsel completion required

Before live use, counsel must finalize governing law/venue, warranties, liability limitations, indemnification, dispute procedures, termination/survival, notices and other applicable clauses. The platform will not mark this document APPROVED until that review is complete.
