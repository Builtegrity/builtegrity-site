# Builtegrity Brand Operating System — V1

Effective: September 20, 2026

## Core principle
Builtegrity is one brand with two visual modes. Consistency means every touchpoint uses the same logo family, color family, tone, spacing, homeowner-first trust positioning, and business-model language.

## Visual modes
### Light mode
Use for website body content, request forms, email bodies, proposal/document bodies, FAQs, and long-form information.

- White: #FFFFFF
- Off-white: #F7F9FC
- Dark text: #111111 to #1A1A1A
- Builtegrity bright blue: #1769F5
- Navy/dark blue may be used as a secondary interior/logo color only.

Light mode means information, readability, trust, and calm.

### Contrast mode — LOCKED
Contrast mode is based on a true-black field, not a navy field.

- Dominant background: #000000
- Primary accent: Builtegrity bright blue (#1769F5; nearby approved blue variants are acceptable when inherited from the existing logo)
- Primary contrast text/roofline: #FFFFFF
- Dark blue/navy: secondary interior/logo detail only; never the dominant contrast background

Use contrast mode for social avatars, social headers, CTA bands, email headers/footers, selected section breaks, and branded callouts.

Contrast mode means identity, emphasis, memorability, and action.

## Hard rules
1. Never replace the black contrast field with a navy-dominant field.
2. Do not introduce unrelated accent colors.
3. Social avatars must be square and circle-safe with 15–20% breathing room and all critical logo elements inside the middle ~70%.
4. Light mode handles information; contrast mode handles identity/action.
5. Keep the request funnel mostly light.
6. Builtegrity must not visually or verbally imply it is the roofing contractor.
7. Public regional scope during Phase 1 is Rhode Island & Massachusetts.
8. Reuse the core process language: Submit → Review → Connect.
9. Quote comparison is an optional capability after review, not a promise of multiple contractor quotes.
10. Never broadcast a homeowner request to a contractor list. One selected introduction at a time remains the default.
11. An additional introduction requires homeowner request or consent plus actual eligible-partner availability.
12. Comparison language must remain neutral: organize documented scope, materials, allowances, exclusions, warranty and pricing differences; do not choose a winning contractor or promise the lowest price.

## Core messaging
Primary positioning:
Builtegrity helps homeowners start roofing projects, reviews the request, organizes documented differences between roofing quotes when requested, and coordinates controlled roofing-company introductions.

Process:
Submit → Review → Connect

Regional scope:
Rhode Island & Massachusetts

Business-model clarity:
Builtegrity is an independent roofing project intake, comparison-support, matching and coordination service. Builtegrity is not the roofing contractor.

## Channel system
- Website: 80–90% light; 10–20% contrast.
- Request funnel: mostly light.
- Facebook/Instagram avatars: contrast.
- LinkedIn: light/professional with contrast accents.
- Email: black contrast header/footer with a light body.
- Proposals/docs: mostly light with black contrast anchors.
- Social feed: balanced light educational cards, black contrast CTA cards, and mixed carousels.

## Comparison capability — September 20, 2026 update

Builtegrity may help homeowners compare quotes they already possess. This is an organizational service, not a technical verification or contractor recommendation. The homeowner remains the decision-maker.

Approved homeowner options in the request flow:
- Find me a roofing company
- Help me compare quotes I already have
- I’d like to discuss another roofing option
- I’m not sure yet

Additional contractor options are intentionally controlled. Builtegrity may consider a second introduction only when requested or consented to and when an eligible partner is available. No broad lead blast.
